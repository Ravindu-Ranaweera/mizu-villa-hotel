The house

Built the old way, and kept cool by it

Lime-washed walls, a pitched clay-tile roof and a polished timber ceiling running the length of the house. The construction is the air conditioning. A verandah wraps the front on carved white columns, wide enough to eat under, and the checkerboard forecourt runs out to a gravel drive lined with palms.

Inside, rooms open into each other through tall panelled doors, all on one level. The garden is walled on three sides and lit after dark, so the evening hours are as usable as the afternoon ones.

Bedroom

King bed under a pale timber ceiling, air conditioning, writing desk and an antique almirah. Fresh linen on arrival.

Living room

Two facing sofas, a low teak table and a television. Air-conditioned, with doors through to the verandah.

Kitchen

Gas hob, fridge-freezer, kettle, blender, rice cooker and a washing machine. Enough to cook properly, not just make coffee.

Bathroom

Walk-in rain shower with a hand spray, basin set on a timber counter, hot water throughout.

Verandah

Lantern-lit after dark, with planter chairs and a table. The best room in the house most evenings.

Garden & drive

Walled lawn, mature palms and frangipani, and a gravel drive with room for a car right at the door.
